Version 1.5

Wireless ADB:
- Full ADB client: pair and connect to any device over Wi-Fi
- Run shell commands on the connected device without a computer
- Saved devices with one-tap reconnect and optional auto-reconnect
- Client ADB key is stored once, so pairing survives app updates

Color themes:
- 5 accent palettes (Teal Navy, Sapphire, Emerald, Copper, Slate) next to Material You dynamic color
- Theme choice is saved and applied instantly

Also in this release:
- Fixed Logcat: the log stream stayed empty because of a broken level check in the parser
- Terminal: pin favorite commands and reuse them as chips
- Apps: filter by system/user and enabled/disabled state, plus a select-all button
