Version 1.4

Battery:
- New battery card with a circular charge gauge colored by charge level
- Charge status, health and power source shown as badges
- Capacity and charge counter in the details row

Also in this release:
- Fixed ADB command normalization for `adb shell` commands
- NFC and mobile data toggles, night mode and brightness quick actions
- Display density and size controls
- App Ops permission manager
- Extended diagnostics report (CPU, thermal, sensors, network stats)
- Command dictionary with autocompletion in the terminal
- State-aware ANSI color parser
- Shizuku binder state tracking without polling
